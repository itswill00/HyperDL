🔥 HyperDL v1.3.21

Quick hotfix update fixing YouTube resolution selection, bot-detection checkpoint bypass, and Shorts format matching.

✨ What's New & Fixed:
• YouTube Resolution Picker: Fixed resolution detection failing when session cookies expire or get flagged by bot checkpoints ("page needs to be reloaded").
• Automatic Fallback: Automatically retries without cookies if Google/YouTube blocks the request, ensuring stream detection and downloads always succeed.
• YouTube Shorts & Portrait Support: Improved resolution selector to accurately match vertical video dimensions (720p, 1080p, etc.).
• Cleaner Stream Probing: The resolution sheet now shows a focused loading state while scanning streams, revealing the exact format options once ready.
• Instant In-App OTA: Update directly inside WebUI (~184 KB) without rebooting your device.
