🔥 HyperDL v1.2.0

A clean, standalone media downloader root module for KernelSU, APatch, and Magisk. Everything runs 100% locally on your device with built-in Python and FFmpeg (no Termux or extra setup required).

✨ What's Working:
• YouTube: instant 144p–4K resolution picker + FLAC HD audio
• TikTok: watermark-free video, audio, and photo slides
• Twitter / X: videos, GIFs, and full-res photos
• Reddit & Pinterest: videos and image galleries
• WebUI: Material 3 design, download cancel button, and multi-select checkboxes for batch delete

📌 Notes:
• Instagram photos and private posts require your cookies in the WebUI "Cookies" tab to get past Meta login walls.
